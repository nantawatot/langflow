# Master Taxonomy for Sports
This document outlines the master taxonomy for sports, including categories, subcategories, and attributes relevant

### Core Attributes
* **Sport Category**: The broadest classification, identifying the type of sport. Examples include Ball Sports, Combat Sports, Water Sports, and Motorsports. This attribute helps to immediately place the event within a general athletic domain.
* **Sport**: The specific game or activity being played, such as Basketball, Tennis, or Soccer. This is a fundamental identifier that narrows down the category.
* **Competition Type**: The format of the event. This can range from a single game to a full tournament. Examples include a single match, a knockout stage, a round-robin league, or a stage race.
* **Event**: The unique name of the competition, such as the NBA Finals, the Super Bowl, or the Olympic Games. This provides a specific identity for the competition.
* **Date/Season**: The time frame in which the event takes place. For a single game, this would be the specific date. For a league, it would be the season (e.g., 2024-2025 season).
* **Venue**: The location of the event, which could be a specific stadium, arena, or geographic location. Examples include Madison Square Garden, Wimbledon, or the Indianapolis Motor Speedway.

### Participant Attributes
* **Teams/Players**: The individuals or groups directly involved in the competition. This includes the names of the teams (e.g., the Los Angeles Lakers) or individual athletes (e.g., Serena Williams).
* **Role/Position**: The specific function of a participant within the sport. This is particularly relevant for team sports and can include roles like Quarterback, Goalkeeper, or Forward.
* **Management**: Non-playing personnel who are crucial to the team or individual's performance, such as coaches, managers, or trainers.

### Supporting Attributes
* **Governing Body**: The organization responsible for overseeing the rules and regulations of the sport and the event. Examples include FIFA, the IOC, or the NFL.
* **Sponsors**: The companies or organizations that provide financial support for the event or participants.
* **Broadcast**: The media outlets responsible for televising or streaming the event, such as ESPN or Fox Sports.

### Example TAXONOMY Tree:
* **Top Sport Category** → Sport categories, such as Ball Sports, Combat Sports, Motor Sports, Endurance Sports.
    * **Sub Sport Category** → Specific sub-categories, such as Road Cycling, Association Football.
        * **Discipline Type** → Type of competition, such as Stage Race, Stroke Play.
            * **Competition/League Type** → Type of tournament, such as Grand Slam, World Cup, Tour.
                * **Competition/Event** → Name of the event, such as Tour de France.
                    * **Organization/Authority** → Governing body or authority, such as FIFA, IOC, UCI.
                    * **Season/Edition** → Year or edition of the event, such as 2022, 2023, 148th Edition.
                    * **Tournament Format** → Competition format, such as Round-robin, Knockout Stage, Double-elimination.
                    * **Court Surface** → Playing surface, such as Grass, Clay, Hard Court.
                    * **Division/Category/Bracket** → Categories or divisions, such as Men's/Women's bracket, U-20/U-17.
                        * **Stage/Match/Round** → Stage or round of the competition, such as Group Stage, Stage 1, Round 4.
                            * **Venue** → Location of the event, such as Lusail Stadium, Circuit de Monaco.
                        * **Team/Player Group** → Name of the team, athletes, or groups, such as Brazil, Red Bull Racing.
                            * **Athlete/Driver/Player** → Name of the individual athletes or drivers, such as Lionel Messi, Max Verstappen.
                                * **Role/Position** → Role or position within the team or sport, such as Goalkeeper, Driver.
                                * **Equipment/Vehicle** → Equipment or vehicle used in the competition, such as Bianchi Bike, RB19.
                                * **Athlete Ranking** → Athlete rankings, such as BWF World Ranking.
                                * **Time/Record** → Performance times or records, such as World Record, Personal Best.
                                * **Title/Championship** → Titles or awards, such as PBA World Championship, World Cup of Bowling.
                            * **Management** → Managers, coaches, or team personnel, such as Head Coach, Team Principal.
                            * **Sponsors** → Financial sponsors, such as Nike, Petronas.
                            * **Team Ranking** → Team rankings, such as MLB Power Rankings, Wild Card Standings.
                            * **Fanbase** → Fan groups, such as "Yankees Nation," "Red Sox Nation.
                    * **Referee** → Referees, such as Earl Strom, Joey Crawford.
                    * **Prize Money/Rewards** → Prizes or prize money, such as $50,000, Gold Medal.
                    * **Broadcast** → Broadcast stations, such as ESPN, Fox Sports, MLB Network.
