Motorsports → Category of Motor Racing Sports
Competitive racing involving motor vehicles on circuits or tracks. It includes various disciplines like Formula 1, MotoGP, rallying, and more.
├── F1 Racing → The highest class of international single-seater auto racing sanctioned by the FIA, known as Formula 1 or F1 Racing.
│   ├── Competition/Event → Official Formula 1 races or championships (e.g., Monaco Grand Prix, British Grand Prix).
│   │   ├── Round/Stage → Sequential events or race weekends during the championship season:
│   │   │       • Round 1, Round 2 – Early races in the calendar.
│   │   │       • Final Round – The concluding race that may decide the championship.
│   │   │   ├── Venue → Details of the race location and circuit:
│   │   │       • Location – Geographic place where the race is held (e.g., Monaco, Silverstone).
│   │   │       • Circuit Type – Nature of the track, such as Street Circuit (e.g., Monaco GP), Permanent Circuit, or Hybrid.
│   │   ├── Team → The racing teams participating in the championship, composed of drivers, engineers, and support staff. Examples: Mercedes, Red Bull Racing.
│   │   │   ├── Driver → Professional race drivers contracted by teams, e.g., Lewis Hamilton, Max Verstappen.
│   │   │   ├── Management → Team leadership responsible for strategy and operations:
│   │   │       • Team Principal – The head of the team (e.g., Toto Wolff for Mercedes, Christian Horner for Red Bull).
│   │   │   ├── Sponsors → Corporations or brands that provide financial backing to teams and drivers (e.g., Petronas for Mercedes, Infiniti for Red Bull Racing).
